Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about zivaBooks operations, licensing, and business features.

Can I manage multiple business branches or separate companies in zivaBooks?
Yes! zivaBooks is built to manage multiple branches or distinct company profiles under one organization. Each branch operates independently with its own sales, inventory, and staff permissions, keeping all business records strictly private and organized.
What is FEFO inventory and how does it protect my profit margin?
FEFO stands for First-Expiry-First-Out. zivaBooks automatically tracks product batch numbers and expiration dates, ensuring your billing staff dispatches older inventory first. This prevents product spoilage, minimizes waste, and protects your margins.
How does customer OTP verification prevent cash collection discrepancies?
Whenever your field staff or cashier collects a payment or delivers an order, an instant One-Time Password (OTP) is sent to the customer's mobile phone. Verifying this code ensures every transaction is authentic and immediately updates your accounts.
How does the 24/7 Dedicated Customer Portal work for my clients?
zivaBooks includes a 24/7 dedicated Customer Self-Service Portal (`/customer_home/`). Your B2B clients can log in anytime to view real-time account ledger statements, download PDF sales & POS invoices, track live sales order statuses, inspect payment collection records, and access active collection & delivery OTP codes without contacting your support team.
What receipt and invoice formats are supported for printing?
zivaBooks supports fast 3-inch (80mm) thermal POS receipts for busy billing counters as well as professional full-page A4 PDF invoices and downloadable Excel statements for accountants.
Can my team use zivaBooks on mobile phones and tablets?
Absolutely. zivaBooks works smoothly across smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. You can also install it directly onto your phone for quick 1-tap app access.
